# Physical Chemistry I

**Instructor:** S.S. Subramanian, Assistant Professor (Sr.G.), Department of Applied Science, PSG College of Technology

## About the course
24S105 Physical Chemistry I covers the gaseous state, the laws of thermodynamics and thermochemistry, and the applications of thermodynamics: free energy, phase equilibria, chemical potential and Maxwell's relations.
